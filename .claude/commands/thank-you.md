# Thank you

Close the current work cleanly. Do not begin another task or make additional
changes.

Return a concise handoff containing:

- the outcome that was actually completed;
- the important files or artifacts changed;
- verification performed and its result;
- commit and working-tree status when relevant; and
- any remaining decision, risk, or follow-up.

Do not describe planned work as finished. Do not commit, push, deploy, or mutate
external state unless the user separately requested it. If a long-running job is
still active, say that the work is not yet closed and identify what remains.

End with a brief acknowledgment, not a new offer of work.
